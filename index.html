02 / Grown-up details

Safety isn't optional.

A sportbike is a serious responsibility. The plan has to be about more than the looks or the excitement.

Before riding

I'd rather wait than ride unprepared. These are part of the purchase plan, not afterthoughts.

  • ✓A properly fitted, certified full-face helmet and suitable protective riding gear.
  • ✓Complete the required licensing steps and follow Philippine road rules and permit restrictions.
  • ✓Practice and training before carrying passengers or riding in difficult conditions.
  • ✓Agree on family rules for routes, weather, check-ins, and when not to ride.

Before paying

No rushing into a deal just because a listing looks good.

  • ✓Get a written quote from an authorized dealer for the exact model and model year.
  • ✓Confirm whether the unit is available brand-new and what the advertised price includes.
  • ✓Ask about warranty, service schedule, parts, and expected maintenance costs.
  • ✓Inspect documents and make sure registration and ownership paperwork are legitimate.

04 / My promise

I want to earn your trust, not just get the keys.

“I know a 155 cc motorcycle is still capable of serious injury. I won't use its size as proof that I'm safe. I'll earn permission through training, patience, legal compliance, protective gear, and consistent responsibility.”
— The deal I'd like to make with Mom
  • ✓First, confirm my legal eligibility and permit restrictions with LTO. At 16, I must not assume I can ride independently; I will follow the exact rules for my age and permit.
  • ✓Wear a properly fitted, certified helmet and protective gear every ride—no exceptions.
  • ✓No speeding, racing, stunts, phone use, riding impaired, or riding to impress anyone.
  • ✓Follow the service schedule, check tires/brakes/lights/chain, track fuel and maintenance, and report problems immediately.
  • ✓Agree on routes, check-ins, weather limits, passenger rules, and a family budget before purchase.
  • ✓Keep studying and make sure the bike supports my responsibilities rather than distracting from them.
